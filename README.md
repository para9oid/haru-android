## Get Mitrio

[Download Mitrio 1.0](https://github.com/para9oid/mitrio/releases/latest). Open the APK on your Android phone and tap **Install**.

**Updating?** Install over your current copy. Don’t uninstall first, or you may lose your data.

## What it does

- Tasks, reminders, daily plans, goals, and habit tracking.
- Talk or type, then choose which suggestions to save.
- English or Hinglish, simple or detailed screens, and different assistant styles.
- Optional AI that runs on your phone, plus backup and restore.

## How to use Mitrio

<details>
<summary>First-time setup and alarms</summary>

Enter a name or nickname, or skip it. Allow alarm and notification access if you want reminders to ring and appear on time. You can change these later in **Settings → Reminders & alarms**. Use **Hear a test alarm** to check it.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Add a task from a messy thought</summary>

On **Today**, type or speak in the thought box, then tap **Find my first step**. Review the suggestions, edit or untick anything that is wrong, and tap **Add selected**. Nothing is saved until you choose. For a simple task, type its title in the task box and add it.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Make a goal or routine</summary>

Open **Goals** and choose a suggestion or write your own. Suggested goals come with a small daily check-in. Open **Routines** to add something you want to repeat each day. In Easy mode, use the shortcuts on **Today** to find these sections.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Talk to your assistant</summary>

Tap **Talk** to type or dictate. Ask for help making a plan or finding a small next step. AI is optional; quick suggestions still work with AI off. If you turn on AI, choose and download a model in **Settings → Assistant & AI**.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Back up or move to a new phone</summary>

Open **Settings → Backups & data → Create backup file** and save the file somewhere private. On the new phone, install Mitrio, choose **Restore from backup**, and select that file. Restoring replaces the information on that phone. AI models must be downloaded again.

</details>

## Privacy and reminders

No account is needed. Your plans and chats stay on your phone. Backups contain personal information, so keep them private. Test an alarm before relying on reminders.

Mitrio is an organizer, not medical care or addiction treatment.
