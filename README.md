## Get Mitrio

[Download Mitrio 1.0](https://github.com/para9oid/mitrio/releases/latest). Open the APK on your Android phone and tap **Install**.

**Updating?** Install over your current copy. Don’t uninstall first, or you may lose your data.

## What it does

- Tasks, reminders, daily plans, goals, and habit tracking.
- Talk or type, then choose which suggestions to save.
- English or Hinglish, simple or detailed screens, and different assistant styles.
- Optional AI that runs on your phone, plus backup and restore.

## Privacy and reminders

No account is needed. Your plans and chats stay on your phone. Backups contain personal information, so keep them private. Allow alarm and notification access for reminders, and test an alarm before relying on it.

Mitrio is an organizer, not medical care or addiction treatment.
